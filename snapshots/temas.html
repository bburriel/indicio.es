Where the data come from

INE: Household Income Distribution Atlas 2015–2024 (the years 2015–2023 through the open project ineAtlas.data). INE: municipal register 1996–2025. INE: Population and Housing Census 2021 and annual population censuses 2021–2025. INE: Migration and Change of Residence Statistics 2021–2024. INE: Rental Housing Price Index (rents declared to the tax authority), by municipality and, by age of the contract, by province. INE: measurement of tourist dwellings (August 2020 to May 2026). INE: Wage Structure Survey. INE: IPC. SEPE: registered unemployment by municipality, 2015–September 2026. Ministry of the Interior: general elections of 2019 and 2023, municipal elections of 2019 and 2023 and European elections of 2024. idealista: average price of rental listings. Eurostat: monthly harmonised index of consumer prices by food group (prc_hicp_midx), 2021–2025, Spain, Portugal, France, Italy and Germany. Distances to the nearest city and to the coast calculated by Indicio as the crow flies on IGN mapping. INE: Consumer Price Index by region (general and groups, since 2002) and by subclass. INE: Household Budget Survey (average spending per household and per person on food, 2016–2025). Eurostat (national accounts, European labour force survey, public accounts and HICP, since 1995) and the World Bank (development indicators, before 1995). INE: Housing Price Index, by region. Ministry of Housing: appraised value of non-subsidised housing, non-subsidised housing starts and completions, subsidised housing. INE: Living Conditions Survey, households by tenure and income. Eurostat: housing prices, housing cost overburden, young people leaving home, housing investment and households. INE: Annual Wage Structure Survey (percentiles of annual earnings and minimum wage bands). INE: labour force survey, main-job wages by decile (with tax data). INE: Living Conditions Survey, income deciles per consumption unit. INE: Quarterly Labour Cost Survey, wage cost per worker and month, by region. Ministry of Finance: outstanding debt of each town council at 31 December, 2015–2025 (excluding 2017). SEPE: initial permanent and temporary contracts by municipality and month (2005–2026) and registered unemployment by municipality. Social Security: contributors by municipality, scheme and activity, December 2011 to 2025. Ministry of Housing: home sales by municipality and quarter (notaries), 2004–2026. Ministry of Housing: subsidised housing with final certification (state and regional plans, 1991–2025), non-subsidised housing starts (1991–2025) and notarial home transactions (2004–2025).

How to read them. Income comes from tax returns and does not include the informal economy. Unless it says «discounting inflation», euros are of each year. Province and Spain figures are averages of their municipalities weighted by population. For very small municipalities INE does not publish all the data. The exact calculation behind each label («at equal age», «among equals», «explains») is in the method guide, and the code on GitHub.
